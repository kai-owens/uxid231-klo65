About Me

Hi! My name is Kai Owens. I am a second year student at Drexel University studying Entrapreneurship with a minor in User Experience and Interaction Design.

I love to explore new ideas and businesses that can reach large audiences. Over the summer I got into game development where I learned to utilize AI tools to code, design, and iterate on designs. Through my experience in this field I have learned iterative design, user research, and prototyping.

I hope to be able to push out more and more awesome games this term and I can't wait to see where it takes me.

My Games

If you're curious, here's some of my Roblox Games:

  • Country Battles 🥊💪
    • Country Battles is a game where you can fight other players in a battle royale style game. You can choose your country and fight for your country to be the last one standing.
    • I recently was given a major role in the development of the game. I am currently in charge of growth, updates, and maintenance.
    • This game has been out for one month, has 1.2M+ visits, and has 6K+ concurrent players.

  • Clean the Squishies!
    • Clean the squishies is a game where you have to clean all the squishies off the floor and place them onto organized shelves.
    • This game was my first big hit, I ran every part of development, updates, and live operations.
    • This game has been out for 4 months now, has 88.2M+ visits, and had 54.5K+ concurrent players at its peak.

  • Search for the Clover 🍀
    • Search for the Clover is a game where you need to find the four leaf clover in a field of over 20 thousand of them!
    • This game was a complete solo project where I ran all parts of development, updates, live operations, and advertisement strategy.
    • This game has been out for one month, has 130K+ visits.
