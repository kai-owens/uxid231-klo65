About Me

Hi! My name is Kai Owens. I am a second year student at Drexel University studying Entrepreneurship with a minor in User Experience and Interaction Design.

I love to explore new ideas and businesses that can reach large audiences. Over the summer I got into game development where I learned to utilize AI tools to code, design, and iterate on designs. Through my experience in this field I have learned iterative design, user research, and prototyping.

I hope to be able to push out more and more awesome games this term and I can't wait to see where it takes me.
